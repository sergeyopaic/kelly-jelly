# Kelly Jelly v2.7.2 — Forward movement lean

The previous movement rotation was only 0.045 radians and applied only to idle frames. Sprint and dash had no velocity-based rotation, leaving their baked silhouette to define the visible lean.

Movement now rotates the upper body forward around its existing bottom anchor: up to 0.14 radians walking, 0.22 sprinting, 0.16 dashing. The sign uses world horizontal velocity independently of the sprite's facing reflection. Speed is normalized by world motion scale. No sideways lean at rest or for purely vertical motion. Charge, hurt, absorb and level-up celebration poses remain unchanged. Collision, combat, facing direction, terrain and boss rules are unchanged.

192 deterministic checks passed, including nine checks for left/right walking, sprint and dash lean, vertical motion, stationary pose and charge. Visually inspected source captures for walking and sprinting in both directions at mass 300. Exported Windows processes for left/right sprint captures exited 0; inspected the right-facing release capture. Shader-cache warnings reflect the restricted environment; no game script errors. A new combat stress run was not needed for this visual-only change. Human assessment of the exact lean strength remains subjective.
