# Kelly Jelly v2.7.1 — Boss timing, progression scenery, quieter warnings

- First boss is due at 90 seconds of active game time. The boss scheduler is checked independently of ordinary group spawning, so the group interval cannot delay 1:30. Enemy capacity and one-active-boss restriction remain. Subsequent bosses keep a 120-second interval and minimum 45-second spacing after a kill.
- Terrain no longer cycles by elapsed time. First boss defeat unlocks mint terrain even before the reward is claimed; orbit ranks 2 and 3 unlock caramel and lavender palettes. Each stage blends over 1.5 seconds. New runs reset it.
- Decoration grows with those stages: broad matte icing arcs, shallow ribbons and flourishes. Shapes are fixed to the floor, low contrast, without pickup sprites, glow, bobbing, collision or shadows.
- Removed trajectory lanes for all projectile volleys, including the nine-shot boss volley. Dash/ram warning lanes remain. Windup animation, volley timing, projectiles and collision rules are unchanged.
